How rows are matched

  • One row is one person. Rows sharing a company are grouped into a single account.
  • An account is matched on erp_client_id first, then on company name.
  • Within an account, a contact is matched on email, then on WhatsApp or phone number. A match is updated; anything else is created.

Columns

Required company_name, first_name, last_name, and at least one of email / phone / whatsapp_number.

Optional: erp_client_id, phone_prefix, job_title, is_primary, industry, website, address_line_1, address_line_2, city, state_province, postal_code, country, notes, tags. Column order does not matter and common header spellings are accepted.
